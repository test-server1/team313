Fresha & Treatwell vs Custom Booking Tools — The Smart Way to Stop Paying Commissions

Date
July 17, 2025

T oday, many salons and service-based businesses in the UK rely on platforms like Fresha and Treatwell for online bookings. While these platforms seem convenient, they often take a significant portion of your profits through commission fees and restrict your control over your customer base. At Team 313 Ltd , we believe that businesses deserve complete digital freedom — and that starts with owning your own custom booking system powered by AI automation .

Let’s break down the real difference between third-party platforms and owning your own booking tool — and why smart business owners across the UK are making the switch.

Why Fresha & Treatwell Are Costly in the Long Run

Platforms like Fresha and Treatwell are designed to serve thousands of businesses, but that scale comes at a cost. They charge commissions on every booking , display competitor listings side by side, and keep customer data within their own systems. This means your loyal clients are being marketed to by your competitors — directly on the same platform.

Key Drawbacks of Third-Party Booking Platforms:

  • High commissions per booking (often 10%–20%)
  • Limited access to customer data and analytics
  • Competitor visibility — your clients see other salons next to you
  • No AI-powered automation or integration with your website
  • Weak SEO benefits — bookings happen off your own domain
“Every booking you make through a third-party platform builds their brand, not yours. A custom system turns every booking into a long-term client relationship.” — Team 313 Ltd

The Power of Custom AI Booking Systems

A custom-built booking system by Team 313 gives you total control and independence. You pay no commission, manage clients directly, and integrate your system with Google Business Profile , social media, and even AI chat systems that can handle bookings automatically.

Top Benefits of a Custom AI Booking System:

  • 01 - Zero Commissions: Keep 100% of your revenue from every booking.
  • 02 - Full Ownership: Your website, your data, your customers — no middlemen.
  • 03 - AI Automation: Let AI handle bookings, send reminders, and collect reviews.
  • 04 - Brand Consistency: Clients interact only with your brand — not a third party.
  • 05 - Better SEO: Every booking improves your website’s ranking, not another company’s.

How AI Changes Everything

With our advanced AI integrations , your custom booking tool becomes more than just a calendar. It can respond to clients via WhatsApp, website chat, or even phone — confirming appointments, sending reminders, and collecting feedback automatically. This creates a seamless experience for your customers and reduces manual work for your team.

Imagine a system that books new clients while you sleep, collects reviews after each visit, and sends personalized offers automatically. That’s what we mean by AI automation with freedom . You own the platform — and the AI works for you, not the other way around.

Why UK Businesses Are Making the Switch

Across the UK, salons, clinics, and creative businesses are leaving commission-based booking systems behind. The UK government’s digital transformation initiative is also encouraging businesses to adopt modern technology — including AI and automation — for long-term efficiency. Owning your digital assets is no longer a luxury; it’s a necessity for sustainable growth.

When your business owns its booking system, you gain the ability to integrate other tools — such as accounting, CRM, and marketing automation. Everything becomes streamlined under your brand’s digital ecosystem.

Final Thoughts — Build Freedom, Not Dependency

Third-party booking platforms may look easy, but in the long run, they cost your business more — in commissions, control, and client loyalty. A custom booking system with AI automation from Team 313 Ltd gives you the best of both worlds: digital independence and smart automation. You pay once, own forever, and grow on your terms.
