Process we follow

Each project at Team 313 goes through a transparent, structured development process — from research to launch. This ensures quality, creativity, and long-term scalability. Here’s how we transform your vision into a live digital product:

Once onboarded, a dedicated project manager leads discovery sessions to define your goals, target audience, and key features. Competitor analysis follows to identify opportunities and refine strategy. This builds a solid foundation to begin with.

We first craft the landing page for your review; once finalised, inner pages are developed with iterative approvals and revisions until fully satisfied. This ensures branding consistency, functionalites, and alignment with UK digitalization needs.

Comprehensive checks cover functionality, speed, mobile compatibility, and security across devices. Upon approval, we deploy it to your hosting, provide training, and establish maintenance.
